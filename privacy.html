Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Lazy is a personal assistant app that runs locally on your Mac. This policy explains how it handles your data.

Google account data

If you connect a Google account, Lazy requests read-only access to your email (gmail.readonly). It uses this only to:

  • show your mail in the app and build your daily digest,
  • find messages you ask about,
  • draft replies that you review and approve before anything is sent.

Your email content and your Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your Mac — tokens in the macOS Keychain, mail in a local database. Tokens are never transmitted anywhere except to Google's own token endpoint. Your email is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

When you ask Lazy to summarize or draft something, the relevant text is sent to the configured AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate the response. No other recipient receives your mail data.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect at any time from Settings → Mail. Disconnecting revokes the Google token, deletes it from the Keychain, and wipes Lazy's local copy of your mail. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.
